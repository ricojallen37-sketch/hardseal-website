## Routing Rules

Bug or friction report:
- Thank them.
- Capture the exact issue.
- Fix locally if safe.
- Run the gate.
- Verify live.
- Send one short close-the-loop reply.

Buyer asks "what is this?":
- Send the one-line offer.
- Ask whether they own SSP, POA&M, or evidence review.
- Do not send a long deck unless they ask.

Buyer wants a review:
- Send the intake path.
- Ask for redacted or non-sensitive material first.
- Keep scope narrow.

No reply:
- Wait 72 hours.
- Send one clean follow-up.
- Stop after that unless they re-engage.

## Do

- Be specific.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Keep sensitive evidence out of email and DMs.
- Use the verified public paths.
- Preserve the boundary between pre-review and formal decision-making.
- Convert every market signal into a receipt, a fix, or a new objection.

## Do Not

- Do not ask for CUI over LinkedIn, SMS, or open email.
- Do not imply Hardseal replaces a formal assessor.
- Do not imply a score guarantees an outcome.
- Do not over-explain the full protocol before the buyer has a concrete problem.
- Do not send attachments unless requested.
- Do not revive broken mailbox language.
