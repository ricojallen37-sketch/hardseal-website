The diagnostic

Four dispositions. Every conclusion stays inside the evidence boundary.

SUPPORTED

The supplied evidence supports the bounded statement, with an exact source locator.

CONTRADICTED

The supplied evidence conflicts with the bounded statement, with an exact locator.

UNAVAILABLE

The required fact is not established by the supplied evidence.

OUT OF SCOPE

The statement exceeds the approved diagnostic question or authority.

Workflow

A bounded post-event review.

  1. Freeze one factual question and the authorized artifact inventory.
  2. Record SHA-256 identities and evaluate only reviewed evidence formats.
  3. Assign bounded dispositions with source-file and field or line locators.
  4. Build a deterministic package containing the record, report, manifest, claim boundary, and recipient-run verifier.
  5. Refuse modified, missing, extra, or structurally invalid package contents.
