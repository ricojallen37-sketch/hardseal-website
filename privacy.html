Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy describes how Hardseal collects, uses, and protects information when you visit hardseal.ai or interact with us by email. Hardseal's design philosophy is to collect as little data as possible. This page reflects that.

What we don't do. Hardseal does not run third-party ad networks, retargeting pixels, behavioral profilers, or session-replay scripts. We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use your data to train any machine-learning model. We do not transmit Customer Data outside Customer-controlled environments unless you explicitly authorize it.
Public channels are not regulated-data channels. The Site, public verifier, public downloads, and public email contact channels are not intended for CUI, covered defense information (CDI), export-controlled technical data, credentials, incident data, sensitive vulnerability data, production secrets, or other regulated customer data. Please do not send that information to Hardseal unless a signed SOW expressly authorizes the data category and delivery method.

2. What We Use Information For

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Respond to your emails and schedule calls;
  • Deliver Services you have purchased under an executed MSA / SOW;
  • Process payments via Stripe (Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment information);
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse of the Site and Services;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Browser Verifier — Zero Data Transmission

The verifier at hardseal.ai/verify.html performs all computation in your browser. The packet you paste into the verifier is never transmitted to Hardseal, GitHub Pages logs do not capture it (it is not sent over the network), and no analytics script observes it. This is an architectural property, not a policy promise — the JavaScript source is readable from the page.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The Site does not set first-party tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, Amplitude, Hotjar, FullStory, or similar analytics platforms. The Site may set strictly necessary cookies if Hardseal later adds login or self-serve checkout functionality; if and when that happens, this Policy will be updated and a cookie banner will be shown.

5. Customer Data (Paid Services)

When you engage Hardseal under an MSA / SOW, the handling of Customer Data — including any Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or covered defense information (CDI) — is governed by the executed agreement and the Data Processing Addendum (DPA). Customer Data remains your property. Hardseal Edge and Hardseal Core are designed to operate entirely on your enclave; no Customer Data transmits outside your environment unless you expressly authorize it.

Customer Data, including any CUI or covered defense information, is handled only under an executed MSA / SOW / DPA. Unless the signed SOW says otherwise, Hardseal does not collect, store, transmit, or process Customer Data outside the Customer-controlled environment. CUI and covered defense information may be provided to Hardseal only when a signed SOW expressly authorizes that data category and delivery method.

6. AI and Model Training

Hardseal does not use Customer Data, verifier inputs, CUI, covered defense information, or customer artifacts to train machine-learning models, and will not provide such data to third-party AI tools for training or model improvement without written authorization in the applicable SOW.

7. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Service providers performing operational functions on our behalf (e.g., GitHub for hosting, Stripe for payments). These providers are bound by their own privacy obligations.
  • Legal authorities when required by law or court order. We will provide notice where legally permitted.
  • Successor entities in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Hardseal uses service providers only for operational functions. Hardseal will not authorize any service provider to store, process, or transmit Customer Data, CUI, or covered defense information unless the applicable SOW / DPA permits the provider and the provider is subject to appropriate written confidentiality, security, and data-protection obligations.

8. Data Retention

We retain email correspondence and prospect-engagement records for up to 24 months after the last interaction, after which we delete or anonymize them, except where longer retention is required by law or by an active or recently terminated MSA. Customer Data retention, deletion, and return are governed by the applicable SOW and DPA; absent a different period in the SOW, Hardseal will delete or return Customer Data in its possession or control within 30 days of termination or written request, except where retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for dispute, security, audit, or legal-hold purposes.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email rico@hardseal.ai with the subject line "Privacy request."

10. Children

The Site and Services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Users

The Site is operated from the United States. By using the Site, you consent to the processing of your information in the U.S. We do not currently offer Services to or from EU/UK markets; if and when we do, this Policy will be updated to address GDPR/UK-GDPR terms.

12. Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet transmission or storage is 100% secure; we encourage you to use strong passwords and to be cautious about the information you share by email.

If Hardseal becomes aware of a Security Incident involving Customer Data in Hardseal-controlled systems, Hardseal will notify the affected customer without undue delay using the contact information in the applicable agreement. For CUI or covered defense information engagements, the applicable SOW / DPA specifies escalation contacts, preservation steps, and reporting-cooperation procedures; the customer remains responsible for any required government, prime-contractor, assessor, or regulator notifications unless the SOW expressly assigns a reporting task to Hardseal.

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
